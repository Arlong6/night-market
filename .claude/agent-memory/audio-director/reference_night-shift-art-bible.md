`design/art/art-bible-night-shift.md` is the visual bible for 大夜班 (Night Shift). Audio
direction should stay tonally consistent with it — `design/gdd/machine-interaction.md` Player
Fantasy 判準3 and 判準6 cite it directly as the visual counterpart audio must match. Sections
most relevant to audio work (verify section numbers/content are still current by re-reading —
this is a pointer, not a snapshot of the content):

- **§2 (突發事件情緒目標)**: failure/malfunction moments (機台故障, spoiled food, etc.) are
  explicitly "喜劇式的『啊！』——是鬧劇不是恐怖" — light doesn't dim, the malfunctioning
  machine gets a *local* high-saturation warning-red light + steam, window can show an
  exaggerated distant flash for comedic (not gloomy) effect. This sets the emotional target
  for any shared "failure" sound family (burn/overflow/wrong-scan/whiff): comedic, not
  punishing or alarm-like.
- **§3.2 (直角矩形 vs 曲面語彙)**: "曲面＝活著、正在運作；直角＝靜態陳列" — only machine
  families (oden pot, coffee machine spout, microwave turntable) get curved geometry,
  precisely because they're the things that are "running". Useful cross-modal parallel for
  audio: whatever is visually "alive" (curved, animated) should also be the thing that is
  audibly alive (has a Working loop / diegetic sound signature) — curved surface and audible
  loop are the same design idea in two channels.
- **§3.4 英雄形狀**: 關東煮鍋 (oden pot) is the visual hero object — highest saturation, only
  curved primary form, persistent steam/蒸氣動態. Oden is also the one station with no
  gameplay failure mode (`machine-interaction.md` C2, Player Fantasy "永遠不出包的那一台") —
  visual and audio hero status should align (e.g. audio treats it as a persistent ambient
  presence rather than a state-driven cue emitter).
- **§4.3 彩色特權配額**: world-space color has a strict 2-hue quota (interactive yellow +
  warning red only; soup color is ambient and doesn't count). Doesn't directly constrain
  audio, but signals the project's general philosophy of tight signal budgets — worth
  mirroring in audio mix priority (don't let every station's alert compete at once).

**Why**: Keeps future audio-director work — palette, mix priority, failure-sound tone —
grounded in the same "world" the art direction already established, rather than inventing a
tone independently.
